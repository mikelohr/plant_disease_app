Privacy Policy for AgrI

Effective Date: December 6, 2025

AgrI is a plant disease detection app that uses machine learning models to classify leaf images.

Information Collection

AgrI does not collect, store, or share personal information. Images are processed locally on your device using TensorFlow Lite. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Permissions

  • Camera access: Required to capture leaf images for classification.
  • Storage access: Required to allow you to select images from your gallery.

Data Sharing

No information is shared with external parties. AgrI does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising networks.

Security

All processing occurs locally; your data never leaves your device.
